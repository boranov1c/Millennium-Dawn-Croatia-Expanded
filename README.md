# Millennium Dawn: Croatia Expanded

Croatia Expanded is a comprehensive submod for Millennium Dawn: A Modern Day Mod for Hearts of Iron IV, focused on expanding Croatia through historically accurate content and an immersive national experience.

## Features

- Extensive Croatian national focus tree
- Historical and alternative political paths
- Historical events and national decisions
- Reconstructed 2000 starting scenario
- Historically accurate military formations and equipment
- Expanded military commanders and political figures
- Croatian military industrial organisations
- Expanded research and technology
- Economic and industrial development
- Regional and international diplomacy
- Custom portraits, equipment icons and localisation

## Development Status

The mod is currently in active development and is not yet available for public download.
